Know when your engines need service

Torquelle is a service tracker for small engines and motor equipment. Add your mower, generator or outboard, set service intervals, and see what is due or overdue.

Free to start · No account · Your records stay on your phone

Made for the machines in your shed

Torquelle covers lawn mowers, string trimmers, chainsaws, generators, snowblowers, boat outboard motors and motorboats. Each service item gets its own interval in engine hours, tanks of fuel or months, so oil changes, filters and plugs come up when they are due, not when you remember.

What's inside

Four pieces that work together:

Service items with intervals

Attach service items to each machine and set an interval by engine hours, tanks of fuel or months.

Log usage in seconds

Note the hours or tanks you used. Torquelle shows what is due or overdue on one list and sends a local reminder.

Service history

Every completed service goes into a history for that machine, with photos and receipts if you add them.

Templates and seasonal checklists

With Premium, start from ready service templates based on manufacturer manuals, and use storage and start-up checklists for the season.

A reminder tool, not a mechanic

Template intervals come from manufacturers' manuals, but your machine's own manual comes first. Torquelle reminds you when service is due. It is not professional advice, and you are responsible for following the manufacturer's manual and safety instructions.

Private by design

No account, no sign-up, no server holding your records. Your equipment, service records, usage logs, photos and receipts are stored on your phone only. They go into your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) and into backup files you export yourself. Nothing of that is sent to us.

Frequently asked questions

What equipment does Torquelle work with?

Lawn mowers, string trimmers, chainsaws, generators, snowblowers, boat outboard motors and motorboats.

Do I need an account?

No. Torquelle has no account and no server. Your equipment and service records stay on your phone, in your phone's own backup and in backup files you export yourself.

When will Torquelle be available?

Torquelle is currently in development for iPhone and Android, with no fixed release date yet. Check back on this page for updates.
