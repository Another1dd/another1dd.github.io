Chepatapa Apps

Chepatapa Apps is an independent studio building small, privacy-first mobile apps and developer tools. Flare, Kin Compass and Bloomelle are on the iOS App Store and Naveli is in development; Perchly and Publy are private package registries for Swift and Dart/Flutter teams. Built and maintained by one developer, Tsimafei Lemeshchanka.
