Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bloomelle available yet?

Not yet — Bloomelle is currently in development, with no fixed release date. This page will be updated once it's live.

Is Bloomelle a symptom tracker?

No. Bloomelle doesn't log symptoms — it forecasts your coming week from your last period start date and average cycle length, and suggests which days may suit workouts, focus work, big decisions, or rest.

Is my data private?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your device only. We never collect, transmit, or share your cycle data. No account is required.

What do I need to enter to get a forecast?

Just your last period start date and your average cycle length. Both stay editable in Settings, since most people don't have an exact 28-day cycle.

Will Bloomelle be free?

Bloomelle will have a free tier plus an optional "Bloomelle+" subscription for the full week view, calendar, and widget. Pricing hasn't been finalized yet.

Is Bloomelle available on Android?

The initial launch is iOS-first. Android isn't being built yet.
