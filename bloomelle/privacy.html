Privacy Policy

Bloomelle · Effective date: 2026-10-06

This privacy policy applies to the Bloomelle app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Tsimafei Lemeshchanka (collectively, the "Application"). Tsimafei Lemeshchanka is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

In short: Bloomelle has no account and no server of its own. Your cycle details and check-in history are stored on your device only. The Application does send usage analytics to Google (Firebase), and these include the energy, focus, and mood ratings from each check-in, but never your period dates or cycle details. It also sends crash reports to Google (Firebase) and subscription data to Adapty, as described below.

Your Cycle Details and Check-ins Stay on Your Device

To work out your cycle phase and daily recommendations, the Application stores the following on your device only:

  • The cycle details you enter, such as the start date of your last period, your cycle length, and your goal
  • Your daily check-ins (energy, focus, and mood, each rated low, medium, or high, and whether the day's recommendation felt like a good fit) and their dates
  • Your reminder settings (whether daily reminders are on, and at what time)

Apart from the check-in ratings included in analytics events (see Third Party Access below), this data is not sent to the Service Provider or to any third party, and none of it is included in crash reports.

The Application does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or contact details. If you email the Service Provider, your email address and message are used only to reply to you.

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. They are not sent from a server, and no push-notification token is collected.

Because this data lives in the Application's storage on your device, it may be included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup or Android backup) if you have them turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms and your account settings, not by the Service Provider.

You can erase your cycle details, check-ins, and reminder settings at any time with Settings → Delete All Data, which also cancels scheduled reminders. Uninstalling the Application removes its data from your device (copies may remain in device backups you made earlier). On iPhone, the Application also stores a small, non-personal “early user” marker in the iOS Keychain; it contains no personal or cycle data, is not removed by Delete All Data, and can remain on the device after the Application is uninstalled.

Third Party Access

The Application uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — usage events, such as which screens are opened, whether onboarding was completed, paywall views and subscription events, and settings changes. When you save a check-in, the event includes the energy, focus, and mood levels you chose (low, medium, or high) and whether you rated the day's recommendation a good fit. Firebase also records some events automatically (such as app opens and sessions) and processes an app-instance identifier, device and operating-system information, app version, and approximate location derived from your IP address. Events never include your period dates, cycle length, goal, or the dates of your check-ins.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error reports (stack traces, device model, operating-system version, app version) from release versions of the Application, linked to a random installation identifier generated by the Application, used to find and fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config — fetches configuration values for the Application (for example whether to show the rating prompt); this involves your device contacting Google's servers with an app-instance identifier.

Adapty manages in-app subscriptions. It processes a random anonymous installation identifier generated by the Application, your purchase and subscription status and the related App Store or Google Play transaction information, basic device and app information (such as operating-system and app version), and, by default, your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address.

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; the Service Provider never receives your payment card details. The Application does not use advertising identifiers and does not show ads.

The Service Provider may disclose information as required by law, for protection purposes, or with trusted service providers who comply with this policy.
