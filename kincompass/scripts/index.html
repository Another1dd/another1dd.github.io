What to say to a preschooler: scripts for hard moments

Short, practical scripts for the moments that tend to go sideways with a preschooler. Each page gives the exact words, why they tend to work, and where the ideas come from. They come from the script library in the Kin Compass app.

Scripts

What to say when your child won't leave the parkWhen a preschooler doesn't want to leave the park, daycare or a friend's house, shouting "time to go" rarely works.What to say when your child keeps getting out of bedWhen bedtime turns into ten more requests, or your child keeps walking out after lights out, long conversations tend to make it last longer.What to say when your preschooler doesn't listenWhen you've said it three times and nothing happens, repeating it louder rarely helps.What to say when your preschooler refuses to get dressedIf your preschooler digs in every morning when it's time to get dressed, three small changes usually help: let them choose the clothes the night before, give one instruction at a time instead of a list, and connect getting dressed to something they want.What to do when your child has a tantrum in the storeWhen a preschooler melts down in the middle of a shop, the instinct is to talk, bargain or hurry.What to say to your child after a tantrumOnce the tantrum is over, the temptation is to explain, lecture or ask "why did you do that?".
