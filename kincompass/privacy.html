Privacy Policy

Kin Compass · Effective date: 2026-10-06

This privacy policy applies to the Kin Compass app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Tsimafei Lemeshchanka (collectively, the "Application"). Tsimafei Lemeshchanka is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

In short: Kin Compass has no account and no server of its own. Your child's profile, routines, and wins are stored on your device and are never sent to the Service Provider. The Application does send usage analytics and crash reports to Google (Firebase), and subscription data to Adapty; none of these include your child's name, age, or challenges.

Your Family's Details Stay on Your Device

The Application stores the following on your device only:

  • Your child's profile: name, age, and the challenges you select
  • The routines you create, your progress through them, and the wins you log
  • Saved scripts and tips, and your reminder and quiet-hours settings

None of this content is sent to the Service Provider or to any third party, and none of it is included in crash reports. Analytics events record actions, such as creating a routine, logging a win, or opening or saving one of the Application's built-in scripts and tips, but never your child's details, the names or steps of your routines, or anything you type.

The Application does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or contact details. If you email the Service Provider, your email address and message are used only to reply to you.

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. They are not sent from a server, and no push-notification token is collected.

Because this data lives in the Application's storage on your device, it may be included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup or Android backup) if you have them turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms and your account settings, not by the Service Provider.

Uninstalling the Application removes its data from your device (copies may remain in device backups you made earlier).

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use cookies, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies to support functionality, analytics, or service delivery. Where required by applicable law, the Service Provider will obtain consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.

This Website

Separately from the Kin Compass app described above, this website (the page you are reading) uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand visits and improve the site. This paragraph applies only to the website; what the Application itself sends is described under Third Party Access.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by the Service Provider. To exercise these rights, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, contact the Service Provider at tsimafei.lemeshchanka@gmail.com.

Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. To exercise your CCPA/CPRA rights, contact the Service Provider at tsimafei.lemeshchanka@gmail.com.

Third Party Access

The Application uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — usage events, such as which screens are opened, whether onboarding was completed, paywall views and subscription events, and settings changes, routines being created, started, or completed, wins being logged, and which of the Application's built-in scripts and tips are viewed or saved. Firebase also records some events automatically (such as app opens and sessions) and processes an app-instance identifier, device and operating-system information, app version, and approximate location derived from your IP address. Events never include your child's name, age, or challenges, the names or steps of your routines, or anything you type.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error reports (stack traces, device model, operating-system version, app version) from release versions of the Application, linked to a random installation identifier generated by the Application, used to find and fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config — fetches configuration values for the Application (for example whether to show the rating prompt); this involves your device contacting Google's servers with an app-instance identifier.

Adapty manages in-app subscriptions. It processes a random anonymous installation identifier generated by the Application, your purchase and subscription status and the related App Store or Google Play transaction information, basic device and app information (such as operating-system and app version), and, by default, your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address.

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; the Service Provider never receives your payment card details. The Application does not use advertising identifiers and does not show ads.

The Service Provider may disclose information as required by law, for protection purposes, or with trusted service providers who comply with this policy.

International Data Transfers

The Service Provider or its third-party service providers may transfer personal data to countries outside your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, the Service Provider will use appropriate mechanisms, including Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop further collection of information from your mobile device by uninstalling the Application. To request deletion or withdraw consent, contact tsimafei.lemeshchanka@gmail.com.

Data Retention Policy

  • Data you enter in the Application: Kept on your device only, until you delete it or uninstall the Application
  • Automatically Collected Data: Retained by the service providers listed above for up to 14 months from collection (Firebase Analytics event data is currently kept for 2 months, Google's default setting, and Firebase Crashlytics crash reports for 90 days), or as set by their own retention policies
  • Aggregated and Anonymized Data: Retained indefinitely as it no longer identifies you
  • Data required for legal compliance: Retained as long as required by applicable law

Children

The Application is designed to help parents manage routines for their children, and does not knowingly collect personal data directly from children. All child-related information (such as name or age) is entered by the parent or guardian and is stored locally on the device. The Service Provider does not knowingly solicit data from children under 16. Contact tsimafei.lemeshchanka@gmail.com if aware of any issue related to children's data.
