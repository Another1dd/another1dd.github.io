Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your device only. We never collect, transmit, or share your child's data. No account is required.

How do I build a daily routine?

Go to the Routines tab and tap "+ New Routine". Add steps for morning, daycare drop-off, dinner, or bedtime, then set reminder times.

How do communication scripts work?

The Scripts tab has ready-made phrases for tough moments like tantrums, transitions, and refusals. Each script explains why it works, grounded in behavioral therapy.

How do reminders work?

Enable reminders in Profile settings, and Kin Compass will send a gentle local notification when it's time for a routine step. No account or internet connection required.

What is the Win Tracker?

A lightweight way to log what worked — a calm bedtime, a smooth transition, anything that went well. It helps you see progress and stay motivated.

What happens if I delete the app?

All your data will be deleted with the app since everything is stored locally. There is currently no cloud backup or export feature.

Is Kin Compass available on Android?

Currently iOS only. Android is on our roadmap.
