Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your device only. We never collect, transmit, or share your health data. No account is required.

How do I log a hot flash?

Tap the "+ Log a Flash" button on the main screen. You can record intensity, duration, triggers, and notes. It takes under 30 seconds.

When do I see my trigger insights?

The Insights screen shows your personal trigger correlations after logging several episodes. The more you log, the more accurate your pattern report becomes.

How do I delete an entry?

Tap any entry in the log to open it in edit mode. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Entry".

What happens if I delete the app?

All your data will be deleted with the app. We recommend exporting your data before uninstalling if you want to keep a record.

Is Flare available on Android?

Currently iOS only. Android is on our roadmap.
